Five questions to think about beforehand

Not required. But if you want our 20 minutes to cut straight to the answer, these are the places I usually start.

  1. 01

    How does the work you want to improve happen today - who does it, in which tool, and where does it break?

  2. 02

    If this problem were solved three months from now, what would be different about your day?

    Who in your team will own the new process - a specific person, or still deciding?

    What's your rough budget range - not a commitment, just so we don't propose something that doesn't fit?
