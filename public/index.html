Everything a founder asks before the call

How is this different from hiring a Zoho / Monday / ClickUp partner directly?+

We're vendor-neutral. Zoho partners sell Zoho, Monday partners sell Monday. We tell you honestly which platform fits your case, and if it isn't a platform we work with, we say so. The 20-minute call is where we find out which one you need.

Why only one sprint instead of a full roadmap?+

Because 31% of IT projects actually succeed, and the ones that fail usually failed because they were too big. We ship one real thing in 10–15 days, your team adopts it, then Sprint 2 is informed by what you learned. No lock-in, no 6-month contract.

What does it actually cost?+

Minimum engagement is €1,990 + VAT. Most first sprints land €2,000–5,000 depending on which solutions you pick. Licensing (Zoho, Monday, etc.) is billed separately by the vendor at their public prices. We tell you both numbers on the call.

Do you work with my industry?+

We've deployed in real estate, education, logistics, hospitality, medical, retail, finance, construction, and services. If your work is on an Excel sheet, a shared inbox, or a WhatsApp group right now, we can help. If you need something only a very narrow vertical tool does (legal case management, specific medical records), we'll tell you.

What if I don't know what my problem is?+

That's what the 20 minutes are for. Most founders arrive with a vague "I need a CRM" and leave with a specific one-page plan. If after the call it's still unclear, we offer a paid Discovery (€99/h) to go deeper - only when it genuinely helps.

Who owns the system you build?+

You own all your data and all platform accounts - they're registered under your company. The implementation IP (configurations, custom code) belongs to us, but you can take your data anywhere, anytime. Anything we host on our side is a transparent paid subscription you can cancel.

What happens after the sprint delivers?+

Three business days to review and accept. Then your team runs it. Technical support is available at €69/h - only if you need it. Many clients then book Sprint 2 (automations, integrations) or move to Managed Services for ongoing optimization.

Can I pick 5 solutions and combine them?+

Yes, that's what the "Add to request" list is for. On the call we help you order them by sequence (what has to come first) and sprint capacity. You usually won't ship all 5 at once, but you can.

Do you work with international clients, or Georgia only?+

We focus on Georgia because of language, local banking, and GEONET telephony integrations. We also serve select international clients - the call is in English or Russian or Georgian, whichever works for you.

What if 20 minutes isn't enough?+

Then we schedule a second call, or you book paid Discovery. We've never had a founder arrive and leave more confused - the agenda is tight on purpose. If you have a complex multi-system case, mention it on the booking form and we'll block 45 minutes instead.
